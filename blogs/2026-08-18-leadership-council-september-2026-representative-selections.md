The selection process for representatives on the Leadership Council is starting today. Every six months, half of the council terms end. The following teams are up to choose their representative for the next year: Infra Lang Libs Mods We are aiming to have the teams confirm their choices by September 23, 2026, and for any possible new members to be ready to join the council meetings starting September 25th.
